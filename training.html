Q Stamp — Training Walkthrough

Step 1 · Enter your idea

Open index.html. Type or paste your idea, or drop a file onto the drop zone.

Step 2 · Stamp it

Press Stamp now. The item instantly gets: ISO date+time in America/Denver (e.g. 2026-10-13T10:15:00-06:00), a SHA-256 fingerprint, a sequential number, and an ID (QS-YYYYMMDD-####-hash6).

Step 3 · Color code

GREEN in front = you originated it first. If others are known to have the same idea, enter them as color:count (e.g. blue:2, red:1); their colors appear behind yours with counts. Choose a status: verified-dated (independent date proof), claimed (your assertion), unverified.

Step 4 · Q stamp (Qhuehi)

The Q badge records Q + human (who), age, and color: e.g. Q · Jeffrey Schnaus · age · green.

Step 5 · Meaning Mirror

See your original text with tag words highlighted. Below is a cleaned read-back; edit it until it says what you meant, then press Confirm meaning. The confirmation is time-stamped.

Step 6 · Directory & verification

The Directory shows every item with its date. Filter by color, status, or label. Verify re-hashes the item to confirm the fingerprint is unchanged. Copy record copies a verification record; Export JSON downloads everything. Data stays in your browser (localStorage).

Step 7 · Lexicon

The Lexicon tab records terms such as Meaning Mirror with classification (established word / project-specific definition / proposed coinage). Not claimed globally novel without research.
